# Demonstration script — 7 minutes, one uninterrupted route

Rehearse this exact branch first. It is the "cheap supplier, replace the wilted herb, fair first price" route,
which ends at net profit 56 and cash 156 and shows reputation gain. Keep a second branch in mind in case a
judge asks: the wilted-herb route is one extra click.

Before the room opens: launch `Capital.exe`, confirm the window is 1600×900 windowed, press `Home` to reset the
view, and leave the game on the title of Scene 1. Reset between judges with `R` → نعم، إعادة (one action,
returns capital to 100 and clears unlocks).

## 0:00–0:40 — the problem

Say: teenagers meet "revenue", "profit" and "cash" as vocabulary, not as consequences. Our answer is a five-minute
shop where every term is earned by an action they performed seconds earlier.

Show: the street at dusk outside the inherited shop. Open the door (`E` on it): the cauldron glows through the
doorway. Walk in (`W`); Firas materialises from his crystal and welcomes you. In VR, walk or teleport in yourself;
the camera is never moved for drama.

## 0:40–1:05 — honest AI use

Say: AI wrote and debugged code, generated the Arabic glyph corpus, authored the low-poly shop geometry, and
helped us test six financial branches automatically. Show `PROMPT_LOG.md` and one failing-then-fixed compile
error. State plainly: **Firas is scripted, not a live model**; voices are timed subtitles until Lahajati files
arrive.

## 1:05–2:20 — Scene 1, hands on the bench

1. Click the four ingredient buttons (cash 100 → 75). Point out that the money became **inventory**, not an
   expense.
2. Walk to the ingredient table (left of the cauldron). `E` on the red herb, carry it to the stone mortar on the
   small table, release. `E` on the pestle, hold the left button and drag up/down five times.
3. Carry the crushed herb, the blue crystal and the water flask over the big cauldron and release each one
   (each splashes in its own colour). The camera is already looking down into the cauldron: take the paddle
   from the barrel and circle the cursor inside the cauldron; the brew swirls faster the faster you stir.
4. Carry an empty bottle from the bottle table to the brass ring on the bottling stand and hold `F`.
   The ladle scoops from the cauldron, tips over the funnel, and the bottle fills.
   Firas floats beside each station; the item to use next pulses gold.
5. Stop on the ledger line: **تكلفة الإنتاج: 25 عملة**. This is the first time the term exists for the player.

## 2:20–3:55 — Scene 2, price and first sale

1. Point at the market board: neighbours ~40 coins.
2. Choose **30**. The customer is delighted and promises to recommend the shop (reputation +1).
3. Hand the potion to the receiving tray. Coins clink; ledger animates revenue 30, COGS 25, gross profit 5.
4. Answer the micro-check: **إيراد**. Say why: the 30 coins are revenue; profit arrives after costs.
5. The merchant enters: 37 per potion because of the good word of mouth.

## 3:55–5:40 — Scene 3, supplier risk

1. Choose **cheap, 45**. Show the wilted herb on the bench.
2. Choose **replace for +5** (batch cost 50). Say the alternative out loud: using it would have produced one
   cloudy bottle, a rejection, 15 of spoilage, and only two payments.
3. Batch craft: one crushing and stirring sequence, three short pours. Do not repeat the whole loop.
4. Merchant accepts all three; 111 revenue. Then the operating-expense parchment: 10 coins, posted once.
5. Stop on the ledger: revenue 141, COGS 75, gross 66, spoilage 0, opex 10, **net 56**.

## 5:40–6:25 — net profit check and encyclopedia

1. Answer the final check with **56**, not 66. If the wrong answer is picked, the game explains opex/spoilage
   and allows one retry — demonstrate that if a judge asks.
2. Open the final report: capital, revenue, COGS, gross, spoilage, opex, net, final cash 156, reputation.
3. Press `Tab`: **10 / 10** terms unlocked, each with the example the player actually produced.

## 6:25–7:00 — close and reset

Say: the same shop, three scenes, six outcomes; the wilted route ends at 24 net instead of 56, and the premium
route at 46. Highest cash is not the only success measure — reputation and risk differ.

Press `R` → **نعم، إعادة** for the next judge. Confirm cash is back to 100 and the encyclopedia is empty.

## Fallbacks

- If a grab misses, the objective line names the next physical action; the assistance buttons under the
  objective complete one stroke/turn/pour without skipping any decision.
- If the headset is available but untested, run the desktop route on stage and say so. Do not demo an
  unverified VR build.
- If time runs short, cut Scene 1's ingredient purchase narration, never the crushing/stirring/bottling moment.
