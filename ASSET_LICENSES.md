## Not used / deliberately avoided

- No Mixamo, Sketchfab or Asset Store downloads: their terms differ per asset and need accounts.
- KayKit licence files are kept beside the models (`Assets/ThirdParty/KayKit/LICENSE-*.txt`).
- No scraped web images, no third-party music, no Lahajati voice files (not supplied).
- No fonts beyond Amiri; no icon packs.
